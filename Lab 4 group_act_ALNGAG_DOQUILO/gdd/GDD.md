1. High-Concept Statement
  Zombie Horde: City Outbreak is a sandbox zombie survival simulation where the player takes the role of a city commander and deploys military forces to protect civilians 
and important locations. Zombies can attack from different directions, so the player must constantly watch the city, move troops, and react to changing threats. 
The main appeal is feeling like a commander trying to keep a city alive during an unpredictable zombie outbreak.


2. Formal Elements
Players
  The game is a single-player game. The player acts as the commander of the city's defensive forces and controls the deployment and movement of military units.

Objectives
  The main objective is to protect as many civilians as possible and survive for as long as possible against increasingly dangerous zombie waves. The player also tries to 
reduce military losses and keep important areas of the city protected.

Rules
  The player can only deploy units when enough resources are available. Military units can move around the city and attack zombies, but they can also be damaged or destroyed. 
Zombies are controlled by the game and attack civilians, military units, and available targets. The player cannot directly control zombies.

Procedures
  The player begins by observing the city and checking the locations of civilians, available troops, and important defensive areas. When zombies appear, the player deploys 
or moves troops toward the threatened area. During the attack, the player watches the battlefield and moves units when another area becomes threatened. After the attack, 
the player checks civilian and military losses, manages resources, and prepares for the next wave.

Resources
  The main resources are military units, unit health, deployment resources, defensive structures, and combat capacity. Civilians are also an important limited resource 
because losing civilians makes the overall situation worse.

Conflict
  The main conflict is between the player's military forces and the attacking zombie hordes. The player must also deal with limited resources, multiple attack directions, 
vulnerable civilians, and military units that can be damaged or destroyed.

Boundaries
  The game takes place within the city map. Zombies enter from the edges of the map, while civilians, military units, and defenses operate within the playable city 
area. The game focuses on surviving the outbreak inside this city.

Outcome
  The game is based on survival rather than a fixed final victory. The player's performance can be measured by how many waves are survived, how many civilians remain alive, 
how many military units are lost, and how long the city remains protected.


3. Core Loop
  The core gameplay loop starts with the player observing the city and checking where civilians, military units, and defenses are located. The player then watches for incoming 
zombies and identifies which part of the city is under threat. Once a threat appears, the player deploys or repositions military units toward that area. The units then fight the 
zombies while the player continues watching the battlefield. As the attack develops, the player may need to move troops to another area, especially when zombies attack from a 
different direction or civilians become endangered. After the attack, the player checks the damage, civilian losses, military losses, and remaining resources. The player then prepares
the defenses for the next wave. A new zombie wave begins, potentially from a different direction, and the player repeats the same process.

Core Loop Summary
  Observe the city → Detect zombie threat → Deploy troops → Defend civilians → Monitor the battlefield → Reposition troops → Check losses → Prepare for next wave → Repeat
  This loop matches the game's main idea because zombies can attack from different directions, requiring the player to continuously observe the battlefield and make new tactical 
decisions instead of following one fixed strategy.
